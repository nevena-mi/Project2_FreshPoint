SAVE Navigating the shift to AI product management isn't just about understanding the technology; it's about mastering the people and processes around it. 

In my journey, I've realized that many aspiring AI PMs focus solely on the tech, overlooking the fundamental product management skills that are crucial. Here’s what I’ve learned:

1. **Solid Product Fundamentals Matter**: You need to know how to write a PRD, define user stories, and analyze metrics before diving into AI specifics. AI is a tool, not the whole toolkit.

2. **Communication Is Key**: A great AI PM can translate complex technical ideas into compelling narratives that resonate with both technical teams and stakeholders. If you can’t tell a story that gets buy-in, the best tech will go unused.

3. **Data Literacy Over Coding**: It’s not about being the best coder in the room; it’s about understanding what data drives your AI and making informed decisions on what to build and why.

As I transition into this space, I’m reminded: the hardest part isn’t the tech, it’s getting everyone aligned on the vision.
