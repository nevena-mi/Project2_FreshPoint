## Accessibility notes
- White text on the deep purple (`#6F52A3`) passes WCAG AA for normal
  body text (contrast ratio ~6.2:1).
- White text on the brighter blue (`#6C7DE1`) only passes for large/bold
  text (~3.7:1, meets the 3:1 threshold for large text but not normal
  body copy) — this matches how your banner actually uses it: white text
  only appears over the bolder, larger headline area, not as body copy.
- Near-black (`#0A0A0A`) on the coral pink (`#E8829D`) or on white both
  give strong, safe contrast for body text (~7.9:1 and ~20.5:1).
- If using the coral accent for diagrams or UI elements: keep it for
  highlights only, not large background fields, so it doesn't compete
  with the blue/purple as the dominant palette.
